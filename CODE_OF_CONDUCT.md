# Code of conduct

niwa follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). This policy sets the standards for niwa’s community spaces and explains how the project enforces them.

## Treat people with respect

Help make participation safe and welcoming for people of every background, identity, experience level, and ability.

Expected conduct includes:

- Show empathy and respect when people disagree
- Give specific, constructive feedback about the work
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and repair their impact
- Respect people’s privacy and stated boundaries
- Keep discussion focused on the project and its community

Unacceptable conduct includes:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or intimidation
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention
- Insults, trolling, personal attacks, or sustained disruption
- Publishing private information without explicit permission
- Conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Follow the policy in every project space

This policy applies to repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other official niwa spaces. It also applies when someone represents niwa through an official account or at an event.

## Report conduct privately

Email [dev@saru.id](mailto:dev@saru.id) to report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable conduct. Do not put sensitive details in a public issue.

Include the relevant links, dates, participants, and any supporting context. The maintainer will protect the reporter’s privacy, review the available evidence, and respond proportionately.

Possible actions include removing content, issuing a warning, limiting participation, or permanently excluding someone from project spaces. The maintainer will explain moderation decisions when doing so does not expose or endanger the people involved.
